Project Polaris

Autonomy with a chain of command.

A PX4 quadcopter flies a simulated Antarctic survey. Navigation proposes each move, perception and avoidance may revise it, and a deterministic Safety Supervisor has the final word before anything reaches the autopilot. A vision-language model watches the whole time, but it can never steer.
