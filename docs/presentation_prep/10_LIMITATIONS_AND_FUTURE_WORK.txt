PROJECT POLARIS LIMITATIONS AND FUTURE WORK
===========================================

CURRENT CLAIM BOUNDARY
----------------------
Polaris currently demonstrates a safety-layered, bounded, single-vehicle
autonomy architecture in PX4/Gazebo simulation. It does not demonstrate
production airworthiness, physical Antarctic operation, or statistical field
reliability.

ENGINEERING LIMITATIONS
-----------------------
1. Python asyncio is not hard real-time. The runtime measures loop elapsed time
   and deadline misses, but provides no real-time scheduling guarantee.

2. The integrated architecture assumes one vehicle, one RGB camera, one MAVSDK
   owner, and one mission process.

3. Navigation state is assembled from asynchronous telemetry and perception.
   The control loop snapshots it, but there is no formal lock-step estimator or
   timestamp-synchronized multi-sensor fusion backend.

4. VLM requires optional Ollama/model infrastructure, is disabled by default,
   serializes inference, and can drop superseded pending frames.

5. Artifacts retain compressed sampled records rather than raw RGB, dense depth,
   synchronized PX4 ULog, Gazebo logs, invocation flags, or signed provenance.

6. The application-generated JSON has no cryptographic signature or independent
   ground-truth source.

7. No-flight mode validates grounded perception and decision construction, not
   airborne command behavior.

RESEARCH LIMITATIONS
--------------------
1. Depth Anything produces relative pseudo-depth. It is not calibrated obstacle
   distance or target range.

2. Reactive avoidance consumes a coarse 3x3 risk representation, not a metric
   occupancy map or globally optimized trajectory.

3. Viewpoint scoring, confidence thresholds, recovery triggers, fusion ages,
   and mission gates are configured heuristics rather than learned or formally
   optimized policies.

4. Active perception is bounded hover/yaw. It cannot perform translational
   next-best-view exploration.

5. Bounded replanning inserts a nearby staging waypoint. It is not global route
   search and does not prove collision-free reachability.

6. Semantic fusion uses fixed windows and evidence rules rather than calibrated
   probabilistic belief. Mixed positive and negative target evidence can become
   conflicted, but confidence is not a field-calibrated probability.

7. Semantic outputs intentionally have zero control authority. This is a safety
   strength for evaluation, but means semantics cannot yet adapt the mission.

8. Observation completion verifies deterministic conditions near a configured
   waypoint. It does not itself prove that the expected physical object exists.

9. One retained success and one retained abort do not establish a reliability
   rate, generalization, or robustness distribution.

SIMULATION LIMITATIONS
----------------------
1. CollisionMonitor consumes Gazebo contact data; there is no corresponding
   hardware collision-sensor adapter in the current architecture.

2. Controlled collision injection is labelled test evidence. It must not be
   presented as a native physical impact.

3. The Antarctic world is a bounded simulator environment, not a photorealistic
   or scientifically calibrated replica of field terrain, lighting, wind, and
   sensor degradation.

4. Collision count zero means no collision was recorded by the application
   monitor. It is not independent certification of clearance.

5. Landing confirmation comes from application telemetry, not external motion
   capture or independent ground truth.

6. The full mission's landing-zone target is explicitly surrogate staging
   before an unvalidated gate. A completed mission does not prove gate traversal
   or landing on the depicted pad.

7. The path-length metric sums sampled horizontal NED motion. It is a proxy, not
   exact three-dimensional distance traveled.

FUTURE ENGINEERING WORK
-----------------------
- Persist invocation metadata, Ego-Context, fused semantic evidence, semantic
  validation, and exact software/configuration revisions in artifacts.
- Add synchronized RGB/depth/ULog references and artifact integrity hashes.
- Separate hard timing and command enforcement into a real-time-capable process
  or lower-level implementation with measured end-to-end deadlines.
- Add explicit process health supervision and fault injection across worker,
  transport, storage, and shutdown boundaries.
- Support multiple calibrated cameras and explicit multi-vehicle command
  ownership without weakening the single-final-gate invariant.

FUTURE RESEARCH WORK
--------------------
- Fuse calibrated stereo or LiDAR with monocular cues for metric risk and
  uncertainty-aware occupancy mapping.
- Add global collision-aware planning and local trajectory validation.
- Evaluate translational next-best-view planning with explicit reachability,
  energy, and collision constraints.
- Calibrate semantic fusion on a labelled scene dataset and report precision,
  recall, latency, calibration error, and conflict behavior.
- Define a narrow semantic-to-mission interface only after independent safety
  constraints can bound and veto every semantic proposal.
- Run repeated randomized trials and publish success, abort, collision, timing,
  recovery, and observation-completion distributions.

REAL-WORLD DEPLOYMENT REQUIREMENTS
----------------------------------
- Hardware-in-the-loop testing followed by restrained indoor and outdoor flight
  campaigns with independent emergency authority.
- Sensor calibration, environmental qualification, thermal/power analysis, and
  degraded-light, snow, glare, wind, and communication-loss testing.
- Geofencing, regulatory compliance, operational risk assessment, documented
  preflight/abort procedures, and human factors evaluation.
- Independent safety review of command limits, estimator failure modes, landing,
  disarm, lost-link handling, and any future semantic authority.
- Redundant localization and obstacle sensing appropriate to the deployment
  environment; monocular pseudo-depth alone is insufficient.

HONEST PRESENTATION LANGUAGE
----------------------------
Say:
  "The retained simulator artifact records deterministic observation completion
  and a confirmed landing under the implemented application checks."

Do not say:
  "The drone proved autonomous Antarctic rock recognition and safe field
  deployment."

Say:
  "The semantic path is integrated in observer-only shadow mode."

Do not say:
  "The VLM controls navigation" or "the artifact proves the VLM saw a rock."
