PROJECT POLARIS SAFETY SUPERVISOR FLOW
======================================

FINAL APPLICATION-SIDE AUTHORITY
--------------------------------
SafetySupervisor in src/polaris/control/safety_supervisor.py is the final
application-side gate before MAVSDK transmission. Upstream components can
propose motion, but only SafeMotion is sent.

PRIORITY AND OWNERSHIP
----------------------

  MissionOrchestrator
        selects bounded objective/policy
              |
              v
  LocalGoalNavigator
        ordinary DesiredMotion
              |
              +<-- ActivePerceptionManager
              |    conditional yaw/hover ownership
              v
  ReactiveAvoidanceController
        reactive proposed command
              |
              v
  SafetySupervisor
        FINAL application command gate
              |
              v
  SafeMotion -> MAVSDK Offboard -> PX4 stabilization

Safety priority is higher than mission, navigation, active perception, and
reactive avoidance. PX4 remains the physical/simulated flight authority.

IMMEDIATE HARD CONDITIONS
-------------------------
Always checked:

  PX4 disconnected
  invalid risk grid
  non-finite requested or proposed command

Checked while armed or airborne:

  collision detected
  local position unavailable or stale
  camera unavailable or stale
  RGB age above 1200 ms
  required depth unavailable or stale
  depth inference age above 1200 ms
  depth condition CRITICAL
  Confidence Engine mode EMERGENCY
  state age above 1000 ms
  expected OFFBOARD mode lost
  supervisor/control-loop gap above 0.5 s
  displacement above 14.0 m

The hard result is a zero-motion SafeMotion with SupervisorMode.ABORT,
hard_override=true, and recommended_action=LAND.

STALE PERCEPTION IS IMMEDIATE
-----------------------------
Stale RGB or required depth does not enter temporal emergency confirmation.
It is an immediate hard condition while armed/airborne. This is fail-closed:
Polaris does not continue translating while required perception is unknown.

Visual staleness has two paths:

  VisualHealthAnalyzer stale after 500 ms
  SafetySupervisor RGB limit 1200 ms and camera stale check
  ConfidenceEngine can also become EMERGENCY

Depth staleness:

  DepthState stale after 1200 ms
  integrated flight forces depth_required_for_motion=true
  supervisor and ConfidenceEngine independently reject stale required depth

COLLISION BYPASSES TEMPORAL CONFIRMATION
----------------------------------------
CollisionMonitor publishes a latched CollisionState. Unexpected or injected
contact while armed/airborne causes immediate hard abort. It does not require
two depth frames, low variance, or neighbour agreement.

Expected ground contact is classified only when all are true:

  entity contains snow_ground::
  ground contact is currently allowed
  absolute down velocity <= 1.0 m/s
  penetration depth exists and <= 0.01 m

Expected contacts are counted but do not latch collision_detected. Injected
collision tests are explicitly labelled and do not claim physical impact.

TEMPORAL EMERGENCY CONFIRMATION
-------------------------------
Configured values from config/reactive_avoidance.json:

  emergency threshold              centre risk >= 0.90
  required frames                  2
  confirmation timeout             0.8 s
  maximum variance                 0.0016

Ordinary translational motion:
  Emergency centre risk confirms immediately and hard-aborts. The temporal
  two-frame process does not delay ordinary-motion emergency response.

Active-perception-owned zero translation:
  Temporal confirmation is allowed only when the requested command has a
  recognized active-perception source and forward/right/down are exactly zero.
  During SUSPECTED, translation remains zero and bounded active-perception yaw
  may continue.

Fresh confirmation evidence requires:

  nonzero depth frame sequence different from the previous confirmation frame
  strictly newer inference timestamp

Confirmation occurs when:

  two collected risks have variance <= 0.0016
  OR suspicion age reaches 0.8 s

The timeout is fail-closed. It confirms even if too few fresh frames arrive or
variance remains excessive. Neighbour agreement is diagnostic, not a required
confirmation condition.

Confirmation resets when risk clears, ownership ends, sequence restarts,
autonomy is disabled, landing starts, or another immediate hard condition
occurs.

NON-HARD CONSTRAINTS
--------------------
  autonomy disabled
      -> hover without hard override

  Confidence CAUTIOUS or DEGRADED
      -> forward magnitude <= 0.16 m/s
      -> lateral magnitude <= 0.25 m/s

  final command clamps
      horizontal <= 0.60 m/s
      vertical <= 0.18 m/s magnitude
      yaw rate <= 15 deg/s magnitude

  measured horizontal speed > 0.90 m/s
      -> non-hard hover

ACTIVE-PERCEPTION ZERO-TRANSLATION CONDITION
--------------------------------------------
Observation dwell and recovery can preserve bounded yaw through some degraded
perception conditions. This exception does not permit translation and does not
bypass collision, stale state, critical temporal instability, or critical
depth. SafetySupervisor verifies the original request source and exactly zero
translation before applying temporal emergency confirmation.

SAFETY HOLD, HARD ABORT, AND LANDING
------------------------------------
Safety hold:
  Non-hard hover with motion zeroed. Mission may continue if the condition
  clears and no bounded failure is reached.

Hard abort:
  SafeMotion is zero, hard_override is true, mission treats the waypoint as a
  hard failure, and the orchestrator aborts.

Controlled landing:
  Terminal handling performs a 2.0 s final hover, stops Offboard, sends land,
  waits for fresh landed telemetry, attempts disarm, then waits for fresh
  unarmed telemetry.

Landing timeouts:
  up to 45 s for landed telemetry
  up to 5 s for disarmed telemetry

Landing outcomes:
  CONFIRMED
  TIMEOUT
  COMMAND_FAILED: <reason>
  NOT_REQUIRED when never armed/on ground

CTRL+C AND RUNTIME SHUTDOWN
---------------------------
Ctrl+C becomes an operator interruption abort. The landing task is shielded
from cancellation. The MAVSDK server runs in a separate process session until
landing cleanup completes. After the vehicle application exits:

  display closes
  collision monitor closes
  visual task is cancelled
  VLM and depth workers join
  camera unsubscribes
  telemetry and connection tasks stop
  OwnedMavsdkSystem terminates its server

SEMANTIC DATA IS NOT SAFETY INPUT
---------------------------------
VLMResult, FusedSceneContext, semantic-validation classification, and
EgoContextMemory are absent from SafetySupervisor.evaluate(). They cannot
clear, delay, or create an emergency, collision response, SafeMotion, or land
recommendation.

KEY PRESENTATION STATEMENTS
---------------------------
- Collision and stale required perception are immediate.
- Temporal emergency confirmation is scoped to active-perception-owned,
  zero-translation yaw.
- Timeout confirms rather than clears uncertainty.
- Only SafetySupervisor output is sent by the application.
- PX4 remains responsible for stabilization and command acceptance.
- Semantic observers never enter this safety decision flow.
