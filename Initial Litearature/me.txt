Confidence engine v0.1

We will initially avoid pretending confidence is scientifically calibrated.

Instead, create normalized health scores:

localization_health
depth_health
visibility_health
obstacle_health
mission_health
overall_navigation_health

An initial rule-based fusion:

overall_navigation_health =
    0.35 × localization_health
  + 0.25 × visibility_health
  + 0.25 × obstacle_health
  + 0.15 × depth_health

But the worst critical score can override the average:

if localization_health < 0.25:
    do not continue normal forward flight

Otherwise, an excellent depth estimate could mathematically hide catastrophic localization failure. Averages are sneaky little liars like that.

Behaviour policy
Health > 0.75
    Normal movement

0.50–0.75
    Reduce speed
    Increase obstacle clearance
    Avoid low-texture direction

0.25–0.50
    Hover
    Small yaw scan
    Search for contrast or landmarks
    Optionally climb slightly

Below 0.25
    Stop mission progress
    Attempt controlled relocalization
    Return toward last reliable region
    Land after timeout

What we borrow from AutoFly’s depth design

AutoFly does something more sophisticated than simply displaying a depth map. It:

generates pseudo-depth using Depth Anything V2;
divides the map into patches;
embeds those patches into depth tokens;
aligns them with visual-language tokens using a dedicated projector.

Their ablation improved overall success from 44.0% to 47.9% and reduced collision rate from 24.5% to 21.9%. In more difficult scenarios, the pseudo-depth branch produced larger gains, including a reported 9.5-point collision-rate reduction with dynamic obstacles.
